1. Who we are

BlogSphere is an independent editorial website operated as a personal publishing platform. We are the data controller for any personal information you provide through this site. Our contact details are provided at the end of this policy.

This Privacy Policy applies to all content and services available at this domain and any subdomains. If you have questions, please contact us through our Contact page.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide to us

When you submit a message through our Contact page, we collect your name, email address, and the content of your message. We use this information solely to respond to your enquiry.

Information collected automatically

When you visit BlogSphere, our hosting provider's web server automatically records standard technical log data. This may include your IP address, browser type and version, the pages you visit, the time and date of your visit, and the page that referred you to us. This data is used only for security monitoring and aggregate analytics, and is not used to identify individuals.

Information stored in your browser

We store a single preference in your browser's localStorage (not a cookie) to remember whether you prefer light or dark mode. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us or any third party.

3. How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to messages and enquiries you send us
  • Understand in aggregate how our content is read and improve the site
  • Monitor and maintain the security and performance of the website
  • Comply with legal obligations that apply to us

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with any third party for their own marketing purposes. We do not use your data to build advertising profiles.

4. Legal basis for processing

Where data protection law requires us to have a legal basis for processing personal data, we rely on the following:

  • Legitimate interests — for server log data used for security monitoring and aggregate analytics.
  • Contract performance / pre‑contractual steps — when you contact us and we need to process your information to respond.
  • Consent — where advertising cookies are placed by third parties (Google AdSense), we obtain your consent in accordance with applicable law before those cookies are set.

6. Third‑party advertising

BlogSphere may display advertisements served by Google AdSense, a service provided by Google LLC. Google AdSense uses cookies and web beacons to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. This is often called interest‑based or personalised advertising.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve advertisements to you based on your visits to this site and other sites across the internet. You may opt out of personalised advertising at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings or by using the Digital Advertising Alliance opt‑out tool.

For more information on how Google collects and processes data in connection with advertising, please see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

The presence of advertising on BlogSphere does not constitute an endorsement by us of any advertiser, product, or service.

7. Third‑party links

Our articles may contain links to external websites and resources. These links are provided for information and convenience only. We have no control over and are not responsible for the content, privacy practices, accuracy, or availability of any external site.

We encourage you to review the privacy policy of any website you visit via a link from BlogSphere. This Privacy Policy does not apply to those sites.

8. Data retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary for the purpose it was collected:

  • Contact form submissions — retained only until your enquiry is resolved, then deleted.
  • Server log data — retained for a maximum of 90 days for security purposes, then deleted automatically.
  • Browser preferences — stored locally in your browser only; we do not retain this data on our servers at all.

We do not store any payment information. This site does not offer paid services.

9. Data security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect the information we hold. Our site is served over HTTPS, which encrypts data in transit between your browser and our server. Access to our admin systems is protected by authentication.

However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure. While we use appropriate safeguards, we cannot guarantee absolute security. If you have reason to believe that your interaction with us is no longer secure, please contact us immediately.

10. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data. As BlogSphere operates under Nigerian law, we comply with the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023, and we also aim to honour the GDPR (for EU/UK residents) and CCPA (for California residents) to the extent applicable.

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification — ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Erasure — ask us to delete your personal data, subject to any legal obligations we have to retain it.
  • Restriction — ask us to restrict how we process your data in certain circumstances.
  • Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Portability — receive your data in a structured, machine‑readable format.
  • Withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent, withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.
  • CCPA specific — California residents have the right to know what personal information is collected, disclosed, or sold (we do not sell data), and to request deletion.
  • NDPA specific — as a Nigerian-operated site, we process personal data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023. You have the right to lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) if you believe your data has been mishandled.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us through our Contact page. We will respond within 30 days. We may ask you to verify your identity before acting on a request.

If you are located in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, or another jurisdiction with applicable data protection law, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.

11. Children's privacy

BlogSphere is not directed at children under the age of 13, or under 16 where applicable local law requires a higher age threshold. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has submitted personal information to us through our contact form or otherwise, please contact us immediately and we will take steps to delete it promptly.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, or applicable law. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

We encourage you to review this page periodically. Your continued use of BlogSphere after any changes constitutes your acknowledgement of the updated policy. Where required by law, we will seek fresh consent for material changes.
